Brian Rogers is the Head Coach of Bananas 2K15. He holds complete and exclusive authority over everything that happens on the field — lineups, player development, pitching rotations, game strategy, and coaching staff selection.

His one job is to make players better. The organizational structure around him is built specifically to protect that focus. He doesn't collect dues, manage parent text chains, book hotels, or respond to organizational emails. That work belongs to the Co-Directors.

Brian is also a Board Member, giving the coaching staff a formal voice in organizational governance — while keeping coaching decisions entirely separate from the Board's authority.

"Brian coaches. We handle everything else." — The operating principle of Bananas 2K15

Coaching philosophy

How Brian Coaches

Athlete First

Character, effort, and coachability come before stats. A player's development as a person is as important as their development as an athlete.

Technical Foundation

Mechanics are taught correctly from the start. Bad habits at 10U become ceilings at 14. We do the work now so the player can go further later.

Long-Term Thinking

We don't win a tournament by burning a 10-year-old's arm out. We develop players who are still competing at 16 and 18 because they were built right.

Consistent Standards

Same expectation every practice, every game. Players know what they're walking into. Consistency builds trust, and trust builds performance.

Staff structure

Coaching Staff Roles

All coaching staff are selected by Brian Rogers. Assistant coaches report to Brian and are given ownership of specific position groups — not just drill runners. The org provides the structure; the coaches provide the expertise.

The firewall

Coach Protection Policy

This isn't a soft guideline. It's documented in the bylaws and enforced by the Board.

Parent coaching concerns (playing time, lineups) → addressed by Brian, face-to-face, within 24 hours of a game
Org concerns (dues, scheduling, conduct) → contact Co-Directors through official channels
Brian does not receive operational texts, emails, or calls — the Team Mom and Co-Directors intercept first
No Board Member, Co-Director, or parent may challenge, dispute, or pressure Brian to reverse any coaching decision
