For families

Paying Your Dues

All dues payments go to Co-Director Jesica Cargill. Deposits collected at tryouts are applied to your season balance.

1
Confirm your balance
Season dues minus your founding deposit = your remaining balance. Jesica will confirm your exact balance before the season starts.
2
Choose your payment method
Zelle (preferred, zero fees), Venmo @Bananas2K15, or check payable to the org name. No cash for dues payments.
3
Include your player's name in the note
Example: "Spring 2025 dues — [Player Name]". This is required — unlabeled payments cause reconciliation issues.
4
Receive your receipt within 48 hours
Jesica will confirm receipt and send a written acknowledgment. Keep this for your records — it documents your tax-deductible donation to a 501(c)(3) organization.
Financial Hardship

If your family is facing financial hardship, the org has a Player Assistance Policy. Applications are handled with complete confidentiality by the Board. No player should ever sit out because of cost. Reach out to Jesica or Jeremiah directly.

For staff & volunteers

Cash Handling Policy

Strict dual-custody required for all physical cash at any org event. This protects you and the organization. No exceptions.

1
Count together — two people, one must be a board member or coach
Both people physically count the cash at the time of receipt or event close. Never count alone.
2
Log it immediately
Write exact amount, both witness names, and purpose. Example: "$120 gate fundraiser — Jesica Cargill & Brian Rogers — 3/14/2025"
3
Hand to Jesica — same day if possible
Jesica deposits via ATM or bank branch. Org cash is never held by any single person overnight unless documented and approved.
!
Never handle cash alone
Dual-custody protects both you and the Foundation's corporate veil. If you're the only person available, hold the cash sealed with both witness names written on the envelope and hand it to Jesica immediately.
