The organization

How We Operate

Most select softball programs are one person — a coach — carrying everything. Scheduling, dues, parent texts, hotel bookings, and lineups all through one phone. That model burns coaches out.

Bananas 2K15 is built differently. Co-Directors Jesica and Jeremiah Cargill run every organizational function. Co-Head Coaches Brian and Bob own the field. The Board governs. Everyone stays in their lane.

Both coaches have older daughters in high school softball. The org is formally structured so either coach can attend their daughter's game without it being a problem — because the other co-head covers the field and the org doesn't skip a beat.
