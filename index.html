The organization

How We Operate

Most select softball programs are one person — a coach — carrying everything. Scheduling, dues, parent texts, hotel bookings, and lineups all on the same phone. It burns coaches out and produces inconsistent programs.

Bananas 2K15 is built differently. Co-Directors Jesica and Jeremiah Cargill run every organizational function. Brian Rogers coaches. The Board governs. Everyone is in their lane.

The result: a program where the Head Coach can give 100% to player development, families have transparent and professional communication, and athletes are part of something they're proud of.
