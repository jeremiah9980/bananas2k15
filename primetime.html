Program identity

The Star. The Standard.

Primetime Fastpitch is a Texas-based select softball organization focused on developing elite young athletes who compete at the highest level. Our teams train, travel, and compete across Texas and nationally in premier tournaments.

We believe in the whole athlete — developing skill on the field while building the character, confidence, and leadership that lasts a lifetime. From your first tryout to your last tournament, Primetime will push you to be your best.
