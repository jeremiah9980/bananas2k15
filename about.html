The origin

Why Bananas 2K15 Exists

Bananas 2K15 is a Georgetown, Texas select softball organization competing in the 12U division. The organization was re-established in 2025 with a full structural overhaul — same identity, higher standard.

Most club programs are really just a single coach carrying everything — scheduling, finances, parent texts, hotel bookings, and lineups all running through one phone. That model produces burnout and inconsistency.

Bananas 2K15 separates the org machine from the coaching machine. Co-Directors handle every organizational function. Co-Head Coaches Brian Rogers and Bob own the field. A formal Board of Directors provides governance. The result is a program where everyone operates at full capacity in their actual role.

Core identity

Mission & Values

Mission: To develop elite young softball athletes through disciplined coaching, organized competition, and a team environment that builds confidence and love of the game — on and off the field.

Development First

We don't coach to win a banner by over-coaching a 10-year-old. We coach to build players who are still competing at 14, 16, and 18 because they were developed correctly and still love the game.

Professional Operations

Formal board. Documented bylaws. Transparent finances. Clear communication channels. Families investing in this program deserve org standards that match their commitment.

Family First

Both co-head coaches have older daughters in high school softball. The org is structured to support them being dads first — and that philosophy carries onto the field with every player.

Parent Partnership

Parents are informed and heard through structured channels — not informal pressure. The Board gives parent voices a formal path without bypassing the coaching staff.

The org structure

How It All Fits Together

Tier 1 — Executive
Jesica & Jeremiah Cargill — Co-Directors
All executive organizational authority — finance, scheduling, brand, communications, sponsorships, and org documents.
Tier 2 — Board of Directors
Brian Rogers · Cara · Open Seat
Three voting board members. Brian represents the coaching staff. Cara (Bob's wife) represents the coaching household and community. Open seat targeted for the right candidate.
Tier 3 — Co-Head Coaching Staff
Brian Rogers & Bob — Co-Head Coaches
Equal field authority. Lineups, development, strategy, rotations. Family-first coverage model — either coach can prioritize their daughter's high school games. Zero administrative burden.
Tier 4 — Operations Staff
Team Mom · Scorekeeper · Travel Coordinator · Fundraising Coordinator
Appointed by Co-Directors. Game-day operations, GameChanger, travel logistics, and fundraising campaigns.

Bob & Cara — one family, two roles. Bob is a co-head coach on the field. Cara, his wife, holds a Board seat. This means the coaching household has a genuine voice in organizational governance without either coach carrying administrative burden. It's the family-first model applied to the structure itself.
