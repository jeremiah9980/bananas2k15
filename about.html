The origin

Why Bananas 2K15 Exists

Bananas 2K15 is a Georgetown, Texas select softball organization competing in the 10U division. The organization was re-established in 2025 with a full structural overhaul — same identity, higher standard.

The name is the brand. The structure is the difference. Most club programs are really just a single coach carrying everything — scheduling, finances, parent communications, hotel bookings, and lineups all running through one person's phone. That model produces burnout and inconsistency. We built the opposite.

Bananas 2K15 separates the org machine from the coaching machine. Co-Directors handle every organizational function. The Head Coach handles the field. A formal Board of Directors provides governance and community credibility. The result is a program where everyone can operate at full capacity in their actual role.

Core identity

Mission & Values

Mission: To develop elite young softball athletes through disciplined coaching, organized competition, and a team environment that builds confidence and love of the game — on and off the field.

Development First

We don't coach to win a banner by over-coaching a 10-year-old. We coach to build players who are still competing at 14, 16, and 18 because they were developed correctly and still love the game.

Professional Operations

Formal board. Documented bylaws. Transparent finances. Clear communication channels. Families investing in this program deserve organizational standards that match their commitment.

Coach Protection

The Coach Firewall is a real policy. Brian Rogers has zero administrative burden. His phone isn't the org's inbox. His energy goes entirely to the players.

Parent Partnership

Parents are informed and heard through structured channels — not informal pressure. The Board gives parent voices a formal path without bypassing the coaching staff.
