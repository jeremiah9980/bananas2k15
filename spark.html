Brand direction

Bright, Fun, Still Competitive

Spark Fastpitch is the bright-color alternative to the Bananas style: playful and welcoming like Bananas, but with its own electric palette and values-first identity.

The look should feel modern, youth-friendly, and social-media ready — aqua, hot coral, sunshine yellow, and deep navy working together with clean cards, big type, and high-energy messaging.

Culture Code
  • Be loud with effort
  • Stay coachable
  • Pick up your teammate
  • Compete with joy
